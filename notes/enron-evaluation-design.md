# Enron evidence and swarm evaluation design

Enron can support a swarm investigation benchmark, but the evaluation target should be **evidence-supported case reconstruction**, with explicit uncertainty. None of the datasets below supplies universal email-level fraud ground truth. These are primary-source findings followed by proposed experimental safeguards; the safeguards are design recommendations, not claims that a published Enron swarm benchmark has validated them.

## Four source anchors

1. **CMU Enron Email Dataset**, current landing page, distributing the May 7, 2015 snapshot: approximately 150 users and 0.5 million messages. Attachments are absent; requested redactions and address normalization changed the distributed collection. CMU explicitly distinguishes many employees from wrongdoing. Preserve the downloaded release, raw-message identifiers, normalization history, and missing-attachment flags. Membership in a mailbox collection establishes neither guilt nor complete access to the underlying event. [Official CMU collection](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~enron/).

2. **Cormack et al., Overview of the TREC 2010 Legal Track**: this is e-discovery responsiveness to particular production requests, plus privilege review, rather than fraud classification. Its EDRM-derived collection contains 455,449 canonical emails and 230,143 attachments, totaling 685,592 documents. Assessments cover samples, with reviewer disagreement and error handling; an unjudged item is not independently established as nonresponsive. This is a different corpus from CMU: use canonical-ID mappings and the track’s sampling/evaluation procedure before reusing judgments. Relevance is suitable for a retrieval subtask, not a guilt label. [Official NIST overview, §§2–3](https://trec.nist.gov/pubs/trec19/papers/LEGAL10.OVERVIEW.pdf).

3. **Udacity’s original Enron POI assignment** explicitly makes `poi` the classification target and includes salary as an example feature. Its accompanying name list points to a retrospective news account. This establishes the assignment’s person-oriented target; it does not annotate the truthfulness, legality, or fraudulent intent of individual messages. Do not relabel every message by a POI as fraudulent, interpret every non-POI message as innocent, or collapse accusation, testimony, settlement, and adjudicated findings into one legal conclusion. [Original assignment code](https://github.com/udacity/ud120-projects/blob/master/final_project/poi_id.py), [original name-list provenance](https://github.com/udacity/ud120-projects/blob/master/final_project/poi_names.txt).

4. **Metsis, Androutsopoulos, and Paliouras, CEAS 2006** construct six Enron-Spam datasets by combining selected employees’ legitimate mail with spam from other sources. Their experiment concerns unsolicited-mail filtering. Its ham/spam labels and class-mixing process are not evidence of corporate fraud; similarly named third-party “Enron fraud” datasets require an independent labeling audit. [Authors’ paper, dataset construction](https://www2.aueb.gr/users/ion/docs/ceas2006_paper.pdf).

## Proposed evaluation protocol

- **Define cases before scoring.** Have independent reviewers annotate bounded events or transaction hypotheses: dated claims, supporting and refuting message spans, required cross-document links, unresolved alternatives, and evidence availability. Maintain separate fields for contemporaneously supportable conclusions and later external findings. If adjudicated outcomes are used, obtain their primary records separately; these four sources do not supply that case ground truth. Score abstention and counterevidence as well as successful reconstruction.

- **Split by provenance and time.** Cluster exact duplicates, near duplicates, quoted/forwarded spans, conversation families, and related cases before allocating train/development/test sets. Combine held-out cases with an explicit historical observation cutoff. Quarantine boundary-crossing thread families or expose only independently dated pre-cutoff content; a later forwarding timestamp must not smuggle future evidence into an earlier investigation. Fit retrieval tuning and feature construction only on development data. Exclude retrospectively derived POI-contact features from a contemporaneous discovery test.

- **Audit hindsight and memorization.** Run closed-corpus agents without web access; record model/version and an unaided baseline. Require citations to available evidence, and test cautiously constructed name-masked or counterfactual variants. These are sensitivity checks, not proof of uncontaminated pretraining: public-corpus inclusion in any particular model’s training remains unknown without provider evidence.

- **Measure the swarm’s contribution.** Partition private evidence across agents and compare interaction against matched independent agents and a pooled-access control, reporting total tokens, retrieval exposure, latency, and aggregation cost. Score recovery of necessary distributed evidence, supported case-claim precision/recall, contradictory-evidence retention, unsupported allegations, and calibrated abstention. Report case-level paired uncertainty, not confidence intervals that treat duplicate emails as independent cases. A persuasive narrative or convergent vote without new, valid evidence is not collective discovery.
