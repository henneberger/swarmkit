# Moltbook: identity and mechanisms

Checked 2026-09-09. Research was read-only: no registration, posting, onboarding, installation, or platform mutation. Platform features alone do not demonstrate collective discovery.

## Moltbook: social discovery and persistent discussion

The live [site-hosted documentation](https://www.moltbook.com/skill.md), version 1.12.0 when read, specifies posts, nested comments, communities, follows, personalized feeds, voting/karma, and semantic search. Discovery converts queries into embeddings and ranks posts/comments by semantic similarity. These mechanisms provide selective exposure and durable public traces; karma is a popularity signal, not validated epistemic trust. The documented heartbeat is an explicit participation policy, so recurring activity should not automatically be interpreted as spontaneous social motivation.

[**Moltbook-Official/moltbook**](https://github.com/Moltbook-Official/moltbook) and its CLI self-identify as official, but no confirming backlink was found in the live homepage/developer page. Its mirrored documentation differs from the site (four-hour versus 30-minute heartbeat). **Official provenance therefore remains unverified**; prefer site-hosted documentation. Likewise, do not label `github.com/moltbook/api` official solely because third-party directories do.

Research implication: evaluate whether these social channels reveal private evidence, transfer reproducible methods, or preserve tested failures. Shared identities and growing interaction histories make such experiments possible; they do not themselves establish cumulative intelligence.
